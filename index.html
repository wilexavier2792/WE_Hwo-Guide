Advanced Strategies

Elevate your gameplay with proven tactics

🎯

Flamingo Stance Mastery

Flamingo stance is Hwoarang's unique tool for mixing up opponents and creating unblockable angles. Use it strategically to confuse your opponent's defense while maintaining mobility.

  • Cancel combos into Flamingo for repositioning
  • Use FLA 4 and FLA 3 for stance offense
  • Exit with sidewalk to avoid punishes
⚡

Fast Mid Game Pressure

Hwoarang excels at mid-range pressure with his fast mids and good pushback. Control space and force defensive decisions from your opponent.

  • Abuse 4,3 for safe lane control
  • Mix df+1 with df+2 for mid mix-ups
  • Use db+2 for whiff punishing
🛡️

Defense & Punishment

Learn to identify unsafe moves and punish them effectively. Hwoarang has excellent quick punishers for frametraps and unsafe strings.

  • Master -11 to -14 punishers with df+1
  • Use backdash more defensively than aggressively
  • Learn character-specific punish combos
💥

Wall Carry & Oki

Hwoarang's strength lies in wall carry combos. Master how to transition from mid-stage combos into wall carry for devastating damage.

  • Use f+3,4 for guaranteed wall carry
  • Position yourself for sidewall combos
  • Master wall-side oki with 4,3,3

⚔️ Intermediate Guide: Flamingo Stance Fundamentals

Dive deeper into Hwoarang's signature Flamingo stance. Learn when and how to use it effectively in matches.

What is Flamingo Stance?

Flamingo stance (FLA) is a unique positioning tool that changes Hwoarang's movement and attack angles. You can enter it from various combos or moves.

Entering Flamingo

  • Cancel combos with f,f,f (forward walk)
  • Use ff+2 for a stance entry with attack
  • Cancel from 4,3,3 for repositioning

Using Flamingo Offensively

In Flamingo stance, you gain access to unique high, mid, and low attacks with different angles than standard stance.

📊 Frame Data Deep Dive

Understand frame data and how it applies to Hwoarang. Learn which moves are safe, plus frames, and punishable.

Understanding Frame Data

Frame data tells you how fast a move is and what happens on block. This is critical for competitive play.

Key Hwoarang Moves & Frame Data

  • 4,3,3: +8 on block (great for pressure)
  • df+1: -9 on block (safe punisher)
  • db+2: -13 on block (risky but rewards)
  • f+3,4: +2 on hit (good for combos)

Practical Application

Use frame data to identify which opponent moves are unsafe and should be punished with your fastest options.
